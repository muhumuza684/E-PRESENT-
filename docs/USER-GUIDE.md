# E-Presence: guide for lecturers

Open the link your administrator gave you. On a phone or a laptop, it works the same. Keep it bookmarked.

## 1. First time only (5 minutes)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Type your **University / organisation** and your **Lecturer name**. Add a **Logo** (a small image) if you have one.
3. Tap **Themes** to pick a colour, or choose your university colour.
4. Tap **Templates** to pick how your PDF list looks. Tap **Preview a PDF** to see it.
5. Tap **PDF & pages** to choose a cover page, page numbers, a signature line and footer text.

Settings are saved on this device. Tap **Back up settings** to save a copy, so you can **Restore** them on a new phone.

## 2. Create your class (once per course)

1. Open **Home**. Type the class name, for example `CSC2101 Semester 1`.
2. Leave "Students also type a Reg No" ticked. Untick it for meetings, where names are enough.
3. Tap **Create class and QR code**.
4. On **QR & PIN**, tap **Print QR poster** and print it. This QR **never changes**, so you can stick it on the wall or put it in your slides for the whole semester.

## 3. Every lecture

1. Open **QR & PIN**. Type a session name if you like, such as `Week 6`.
2. Keep the suggested PIN or type your own (3 to 10 letters or numbers). Tap **Start sign-in**.
3. Show the screen. Students scan the QR and type the PIN you display.
4. Watch the count go up. **Change PIN** if students start sharing it. **End sign-in** when you are done.

## 4. What students do

1. Scan the QR code with the phone camera.
2. Type Reg No and full name (remembered next time), then the PIN on the lecturer's screen.
3. Tap **Sign in**. A green tick means done.

If they see "Sign-in is not open right now", ask the lecturer to start it, then tap **Check again**.

## 5. After class

Open **Lists**. The newest session shows first, and it updates by itself while sign-in is open.

- **Status** says Present, or Check if two people signed in from one phone, so look at those.
- **Method** says Scanned, or Added by hand.
- **Download PDF** makes the file from your template. **Download CSV** opens in Excel.
- **Email me** sends the list to your email address.
- **Whole semester** gives one table: a row per student, a column per session, and a total.
- The dropdown shows every past session. Each session has its own list.

## 6. Someone has no phone

On **Lists**, under **Add someone by hand**, paste rows copied from a Google Sheet (Reg No and Name, one person per row), then tap **Add to this list**. Duplicates are skipped.

## 7. Printing the PDF

Use **Download PDF**, then print the file. Do not use the browser's print button on the page, because it adds the web address and date.

## 8. Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Sign-in is not open" | Start sign-in on QR & PIN, then tap Check again on the phone |
| "Wrong PIN" | Check the PIN on your screen. Five wrong tries lock that phone for 10 minutes |
| Student says the QR does nothing | Ask them to open the camera app, or type the web address printed under the poster |
| A name shows Check | Two people used one phone. Confirm with them |
| New phone, settings gone | Settings > Restore, with your backup file |
| Cleared the browser by mistake | Your data is safe in the Sheet, but you lose the class on that browser. Ask your administrator |

## 9. Tips

- Put the PIN on the projector, not in the group chat.
- Change the PIN halfway through a long lecture to stop shared sign-ins.
- Print the poster in A4 colour so phones scan it from a few metres.
